Emancipated to follow the Saviour

A mission is more than travelling to another place. It is co-working with God in the great work of soul winning. There is a work that needs to be done and a work that needs to be finished. What a privilege it is to co-work with Christ in this finishing work, allowing Him to prepare us and work through us as we help prepare others to stand true to God. When Christ bids us go, His biddings are His enablings. He will equip us for the work He has called us to do.

By His grace, we are going to Chadiza to share the gospel of His grace, proclaim His soon return, and point people to Christ and His saving power. The work will include evangelism, medical missionary work, community services, literature evangelism, prayer bands, public campaigns, health education and much more.

You too can be part of this noble work. You can support through monetary giving, sponsor a missionary, attend and serve, or simply commit this mission to God through prayer.
